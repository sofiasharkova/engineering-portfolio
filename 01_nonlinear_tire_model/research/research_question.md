# Research Question

## Main Research Question

How accurately can a progressively refined tire model represent the relationship between slip, vertical load, and tire force, and how does model fidelity affect predicted vehicle behaviour?

## Supporting Questions

1. How useful is a constant-friction model?
2. What does cornering stiffness tell us about tire behaviour?
3. How does a nonlinear tire model represent tire saturation?
4. How does vertical load affect tire performance?
5. How does combined braking and cornering affect available tire force?
6. How does increasing tire-model fidelity affect vehicle-level predictions?
7. What are the limitations of the developed model?

## Project Objective

Develop and validate a progressively refined tire model, beginning with simple analytical approximations and advancing toward a nonlinear model suitable for integration into a vehicle-dynamics simulation.
