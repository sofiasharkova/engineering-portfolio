# Mechanical Engineering Portfolio

A digital engineering portfolio focused on mechanical engineering, motorsport, computational modelling, simulation, design, and multidisciplinary vehicle development.

## Engineering Focus

- Vehicle dynamics
- Tire modelling
- Suspension design
- Structural analysis
- FEA
- Aerodynamics
- CFD
- Thermal management
- Controls
- Numerical modelling
- Optimization
- Digital twins
- Systems engineering

## Portfolio Architecture

The portfolio consists of foundational engineering investigations that progressively develop into larger multidisciplinary case studies.

### Foundations

Foundational projects establish the engineering requirements, assumptions, modelling methods, and computational tools used throughout the portfolio.

### Flagship Case Studies

1. Nonlinear Tire Model
2. Parametric Double-Wishbone Suspension
3. Suspension FEA & Topology Optimization
4. Brake Thermal-Structural Design
5. Parametric Rear-Wing CFD Optimization
6. Ground-Effect Diffuser & Aero Platform
7. Integrated Cooling System
8. Full Vehicle Lap-Time Simulator
9. Active Aero / Ride-Height Control
10. Multidisciplinary Vehicle Optimization
11. Race-Car Digital Twin
12. Full Virtual Race-Car Development Program
